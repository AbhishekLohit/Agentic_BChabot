# UPI - Bill Payments

Yes, many housing societies and clubs accept UPI for maintenance fees. You can pay by scanning the QR code provided by the society or by entering their UPI ID. Some societies also have a payment portal where UPI is an option.

Check if your society or club has a UPI QR code or UPI ID. If they have a portal, choose UPI and follow the instructions. If not, ask for their UPI details.

The user learns that clubhouse maintenance fees can be paid via UPI.
