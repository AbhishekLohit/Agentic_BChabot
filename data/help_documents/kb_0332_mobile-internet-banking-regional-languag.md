# Mobile & Internet Banking - Regional Language Support

Yes, if your bank's app supports regional languages, you can change it to Kannada or other languages. Look for the 'Language' or 'Settings' option in the app. Select your preferred language. The app interface will change accordingly.

Open your mobile banking app. Go to 'Settings' or 'Profile'. Look for 'Language' or 'App Language'. Select 'Kannada' from the list. The app interface and menus will change to Kannada.
