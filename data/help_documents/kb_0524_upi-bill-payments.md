# UPI - Bill Payments

Yes, you can pay your DTH or cable TV bill using UPI. Most UPI apps have a 'Bill Payments' or 'Recharge' section where you can select DTH or Cable TV. Enter your subscriber ID or account number, and the bill will be fetched. You can then pay using your UPI PIN.

Open your UPI app, go to 'Recharge & Bills', select DTH or Cable TV, enter your subscriber ID, and pay.

The user learns that UPI can be used for DTH bill payments.
