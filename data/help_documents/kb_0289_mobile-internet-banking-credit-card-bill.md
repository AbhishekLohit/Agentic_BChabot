# Mobile & Internet Banking - Credit Card Bill Payment

Yes, you can pay your credit card bill through net banking. If the credit card is from the same bank, you can directly transfer funds from your savings account to the credit card account. If it's from another bank, you can use the 'Pay Bills' or 'BBPS' section. The process is quick, and the payment is usually credited within 1-2 working days.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Payments' or 'Bill Pay' section. Select 'Credit Card Payment' or 'Pay Credit Card Bill'. If you have a card from the same bank, you can select it from your linked accounts. If from another bank, enter the card details and the amount. Choose the debit account. Confirm and submit. The payment will be processed.
