# UPI - Beneficiary Setup

Yes, UPI does not require you to add a beneficiary like NEFT or RTGS. You can send money to anyone using their UPI ID, phone number, or by scanning their QR code. There is no separate beneficiary addition process for UPI. Just enter the recipient's UPI ID or scan the QR code, and you can send money immediately.

Open your UPI app, enter the recipient's UPI ID or phone number, or scan their QR code. Enter the amount, confirm with your UPI PIN, and the money will be sent instantly.

The user learns that UPI does not require beneficiary addition.
