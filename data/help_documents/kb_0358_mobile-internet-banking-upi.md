# Mobile & Internet Banking - UPI

Yes, you can generate a UPI QR code for receiving payments through net banking. Look for 'UPI QR' or 'Generate QR' in your net banking portal. This code can be shared with customers to receive payments. The amount is credited directly to your account.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to 'UPI' or 'Payments' section. Select 'Generate QR Code' or 'My QR Code'. The bank will generate a QR code linked to your account. You can download and display this QR code for receiving UPI payments.

The user learns how to generate a UPI QR code through net banking.
