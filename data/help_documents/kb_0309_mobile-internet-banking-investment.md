# Mobile & Internet Banking - Investment

Yes, many banks offer the facility to open a Demat account through net banking. Look for 'Demat Account' or 'Trading Account' in the investment section. The process is paperless and uses your KYC details from the bank. The account is opened instantly, and you can start investing in stocks and securities.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Investments' section. Select 'Demat Account' or 'Open Demat Account'. Fill in the required details and accept the terms. The account will be opened instantly. You will receive your Demat account number. You can then start trading through the bank's trading platform.
