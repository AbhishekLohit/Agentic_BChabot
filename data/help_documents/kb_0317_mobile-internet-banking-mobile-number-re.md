# Mobile & Internet Banking - Mobile Number Registration

Your mobile number must be linked to your bank account to use mobile banking and receive OTPs. If it is not registered, you will not be able to activate the service. To register or update your mobile number, you will need to visit your bank branch and submit a request. Some banks may allow you to do this through an ATM using your debit card.

Visit your bank's home branch with your passbook and a valid ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN). Fill out a 'Request for Change of Mobile Number' form. Submit it and your ID proof for verification. The bank will update your mobile number in their system. Once updated, wait for a confirmation SMS. You can then try to register for mobile banking again.

The user learns the primary methods to register or update a mobile number with their bank.
