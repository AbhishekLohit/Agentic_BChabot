# Mobile & Internet Banking - Security

Yes, Multi-factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for high-value transactions and sensitive operations in net banking. It typically involves a combination of password, OTP, and sometimes biometric. MFA is a regulatory requirement by RBI to reduce fraud risk. You cannot opt out of MFA.

MFA is automatic. When you perform a sensitive operation (e.g., adding a beneficiary, making a high-value transfer), the system will ask for multiple factors (e.g., password + OTP). You must have access to your registered mobile number to receive the OTP.

The user learns about MFA, its purpose, and that it is mandatory for security.
