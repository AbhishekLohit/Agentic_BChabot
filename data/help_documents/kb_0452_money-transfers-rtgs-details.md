# Money Transfers - RTGS Details

For an RTGS transfer, you need the beneficiary's full name (as per bank records), bank account number, and the IFSC code of the beneficiary's bank branch. You also need the amount you wish to transfer (minimum ₹2 lakh). These details are the same as for NEFT. Make sure the beneficiary's name matches their bank records to avoid rejection.

Ask the beneficiary for their account number, IFSC code, and the exact name on the account. Add them as a beneficiary in your net banking. Choose RTGS, enter the amount, and confirm.

The user learns the details required for an RTGS transfer.
