# UPI - Insurance Payments

Yes, LIC accepts UPI payments for policy premiums. You can pay through the LIC portal or app by choosing UPI as the payment method. You can also use your UPI app to scan the QR code generated on the LIC portal. The daily UPI limit applies.

Log in to the LIC portal, go to premium payment, select UPI, and follow the instructions. Alternatively, use your UPI app to scan the QR code on the LIC portal.

The user learns that LIC premiums can be paid via UPI.
