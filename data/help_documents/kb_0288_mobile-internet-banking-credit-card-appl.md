# Mobile & Internet Banking - Credit Card Application

Yes, most banks allow you to apply for a credit card directly through net banking or mobile banking. You can find the 'Credit Card' or 'Apply for Card' option in your portal. The application process is pre-filled with your KYC details, making it faster. You can also check for pre-approved offers in your portal. The eligibility is checked automatically, and you may get instant approval if you have an existing relationship with the bank.

Log in to your net banking portal. Look for the 'Credit Card' or 'Cards' section. Select 'Apply Now' or 'Check Eligibility'. The system will show you the cards you are eligible for. Select the card, complete the application with any additional details, and submit. You will receive an instant decision on your application. The card will be dispatched to your registered address.

The user learns that credit cards can be applied for directly through net banking.
