# UPI - Bill Payments

Yes, you can pay your electricity bill without a physical bill. You just need your consumer number or account number, which is usually on your previous bill. Enter the consumer number in your UPI app, and the bill details will be fetched from the electricity board. You can then pay using your UPI PIN.

Open your UPI app, go to 'Bill Payments', select 'Electricity', enter your consumer number, fetch the bill, and pay.

The user learns that UPI can be used for electricity bill payments without a physical bill.
