# Account Security & Management

UPI registration requires the SIM card linked to the bank account to be physically present in the device during setup — it uses an SMS-based device binding. So your wife cannot set up UPI on her phone linked to your account if the registered mobile number SIM is only in your phone. Workarounds include: opening a joint account where both phone numbers are registered, opening a separate account in your wife's name, or using the bank's authorized user feature where one exists.
