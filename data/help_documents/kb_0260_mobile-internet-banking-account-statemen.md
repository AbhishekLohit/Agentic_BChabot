# Mobile & Internet Banking - Account Statements

Yes, net banking portals allow you to download a 'Transaction Summary' or 'Account Statement' in PDF format. You can select a custom date range (e.g., monthly) and download the summary. The summary includes all debits and credits, transaction descriptions, and closing balance. This is useful for personal accounting or tax filing.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Accounts' section. Select 'Download Account Statement'. Choose the account, select the date range for the month, and select PDF format. Click 'Download'. You will get a detailed statement for that month.

The user learns how to download a monthly transaction summary in PDF format.
