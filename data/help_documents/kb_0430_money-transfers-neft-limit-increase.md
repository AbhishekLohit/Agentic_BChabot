# Money Transfers - NEFT Limit Increase

Banks set NEFT limits based on account type and risk profile. To increase the NEFT limit for a one-time large payment, you can visit your bank branch and request a temporary limit increase. Some banks may also allow you to do this through net banking by sending a request. You may need to provide a justification for the high-value transfer.

Log in to your net banking and check for 'Limit Management' or 'Transaction Limit' settings. If available, request an increase. If not, visit your bank branch with your ID proof and request a temporary increase for the specific transaction.

The user learns that NEFT limits can be increased through branch or online request.
