# UPI - Offline Payments

UPI generally requires an internet connection to process transactions. However, NPCI has introduced 'UPI Lite' and 'UPI 123Pay' for offline or low-connectivity scenarios. UPI Lite allows small-value offline transactions using a balance in a separate wallet. UPI 123Pay uses USSD codes and does not require internet, but it has limited functionality.

Check if your bank supports UPI Lite or UPI 123Pay. For regular UPI, ensure you have a stable internet connection. Alternatively, use USSD codes (*99#) for basic transactions without internet.
