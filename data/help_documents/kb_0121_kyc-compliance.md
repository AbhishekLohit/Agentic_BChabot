UPI registration requires a physical SIM-based mobile number because the device binding process uses an SMS sent by NPCI to the registered number to verify device authenticity. Virtual numbers (internet-based SIM, VoIP numbers) cannot receive this binding SMS reliably and are excluded from UPI registration to prevent fraud. This is specified in NPCI's UPI onboarding technical guidelines. As per NPCI's UPI technical specifications and TRAI's telecom subscriber registration norms.

You will need a physical SIM registered in your name under your Aadhaar/ID to complete UPI setup. Low-cost prepaid SIMs from any telecom operator (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL) serve this purpose. eSIMs on supported devices are also accepted as they are registered with TRAI under your identity.
