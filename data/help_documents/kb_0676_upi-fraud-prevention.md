# UPI Fraud Prevention

This is an 'overpayment scam'. Fraudsters intentionally send a small amount to gain trust and create a sense of obligation, then fabricate a story about error charges to extract a much larger amount. Often, the initial ₹500 is sent from a stolen or hacked account and will be reversed later, leaving you at a net loss of the ₹5000 you 'returned'. As per NPCI fraud pattern documentation and RBI advisories on mule account usage.

Do not send any money back. Contact your bank branch and request them to reverse the incoming transaction to the original sender. If the caller persists, block the number and report the UPI handle to your payment app. File a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in if pressured.

User learns the overpayment scam pattern and knows the correct procedure: never return money directly; always let the bank handle the reversal.
