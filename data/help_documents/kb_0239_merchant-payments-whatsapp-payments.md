# Merchant Payments - WhatsApp Payments

WhatsApp has integrated UPI payments in India, allowing businesses to accept payments directly on WhatsApp. However, this feature is currently limited to certain businesses and requires you to have a WhatsApp Business account and a payment gateway integration. You can use WhatsApp's native payment feature (WhatsApp Pay) for peer-to-peer transfers, but for merchant payments, you need to integrate a payment gateway like Razorpay or PayU with WhatsApp Business via the WhatsApp API. This allows you to send payment links or QR codes to customers on WhatsApp.

If you have a WhatsApp Business API account, you can integrate with a payment gateway to send payment links. Alternatively, you can simply share your UPI QR code on WhatsApp and ask customers to scan it. For a more automated experience, consider using a WhatsApp Business solution provider that supports payment integration. You can also use the 'catalog' feature on WhatsApp Business to display products and share payment links for each.
