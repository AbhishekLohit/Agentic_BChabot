# UPI - Phone Number Transfers

Yes, if your friend's phone number is linked to their UPI ID, you can send money using their phone number. In your UPI app, you can select 'Send to Mobile Number' and enter the phone number. The app will automatically identify the recipient's UPI ID if it is registered with that number.

Enter your friend's phone number in your UPI app, and the app will fetch their UPI ID if available. Confirm and send the money.

The user learns that UPI can be used with phone numbers.
