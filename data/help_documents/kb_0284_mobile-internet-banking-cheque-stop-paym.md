# Mobile & Internet Banking - Cheque Stop Payment

You can check the status of a stop payment request in your net banking portal. Look for 'Stop Cheque Request' or 'Stop Payment History'. You will see a list of your requests with their status (e.g., 'Active', 'Executed', 'Cancelled'). This helps you confirm whether the cheque has been successfully blocked or not.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Services' or 'Transactions' section. Look for 'Stop Cheque History' or 'Stop Payment Status'. You will see all your stop payment requests. Check the status of the specific request. If it shows 'Active', the cheque is blocked. If it is 'Cancelled', the stop payment was reversed.

The user learns how to track the status of a stop payment request online.
