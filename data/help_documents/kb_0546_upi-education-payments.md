# UPI - Education Payments

Yes, many colleges and universities accept UPI payments for fees. You can pay by scanning the QR code provided by the college or by entering their UPI ID. Some colleges also have a payment portal where you can choose UPI as the payment method. The daily UPI limit of ₹1 lakh applies.

Check if the college has a UPI QR code or UPI ID. If they have a portal, choose UPI and follow the instructions. If not, ask the college for their UPI details.

The user learns that college fees can be paid via UPI.
