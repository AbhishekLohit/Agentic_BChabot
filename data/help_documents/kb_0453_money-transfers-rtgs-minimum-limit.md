# Money Transfers - RTGS Minimum Limit

Yes, you can use RTGS for ₹2 lakhs as the minimum amount for RTGS is exactly ₹2 lakh. RTGS provides real-time credit, so the vendor will receive the money instantly. RTGS is available 24/7 and is free for online transfers from savings accounts. This makes it an ideal choice for ₹2 lakh transfers.

Log in to your net banking portal. Choose RTGS as the transfer method. Enter the vendor's account details. Enter the amount of ₹2,00,000. Confirm with OTP. The funds will be credited instantly.

The user learns that ₹2 lakh is the minimum for RTGS, making it suitable for such transfers.
