# UPI - Hospitality Payments

Yes, many hotels accept UPI payments at check-in or check-out. You can scan the hotel's QR code at the reception, enter the amount, and pay using your UPI PIN. UPI is widely accepted for hotel stays.

At hotel check-in or check-out, scan the hotel's QR code using your UPI app, enter the room charge, and confirm the payment.

The user learns that UPI can be used for hotel payments at check-in.
