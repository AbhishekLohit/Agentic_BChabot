# Mobile & Internet Banking - USSD Banking

USSD banking (*99#) provides a 'Mini Statement' that shows the last 5-10 transactions in your account. It includes the date, transaction type (debit/credit), amount, and the balance after the transaction. It is a quick way to get an account overview without internet.

Dial *99# from your registered mobile number. Select the option for 'Mini Statement' or 'Balance Inquiry'. The system will send an SMS with your last 5 transactions or show them on the screen. This helps you quickly check your recent activity.
