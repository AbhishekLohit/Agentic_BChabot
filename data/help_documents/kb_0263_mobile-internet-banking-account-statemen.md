# Mobile & Internet Banking - Account Statements

Yes, you can request a duplicate statement for previous years through net banking. The portal allows you to download statements up to 2-3 years back. If you need older statements, you can request them through the 'Service Request' or 'e-Statement Request' option. The bank will send the statement to your registered email or make it available for download within a few days.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Accounts' section. Select 'Download Account Statement'. Choose a custom date range covering the previous year. Download the statement. If you cannot download it, go to 'Service Request' and select 'Request Duplicate Statement'. The bank will process the request and send you the statement.
