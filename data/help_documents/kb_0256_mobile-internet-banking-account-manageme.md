# Mobile & Internet Banking - Account Management

Many mobile banking apps provide a 'Spending Insights' or 'Monthly Summary' feature. It categorizes your transactions into different categories like groceries, food, shopping, bills. It shows you a summary of your spending habits, helping you manage your finances better. This is a value-added feature for personal finance management.

Open your mobile banking app. Look for 'Spending Insights', 'Monthly Summary', or 'My Finance'. The app will analyze your transactions and present a report. You can see your spending by category and month. This helps in budgeting and financial planning.

The user learns about spending insights features in mobile banking for better expense tracking.
