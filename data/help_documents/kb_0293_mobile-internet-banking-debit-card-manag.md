# Mobile & Internet Banking - Debit Card Management

Yes, you can change your debit card PIN through net banking without using an ATM. Look for 'Change PIN' or 'PIN Change' in the 'Cards' section. You need to verify through OTP. The new PIN is activated immediately.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Cards' section. Select your debit card. Click on 'Change PIN' or 'Change Debit Card PIN'. Enter your current PIN and the new PIN. Verify through OTP. The PIN will be changed.

The user learns how to change debit card PIN online.
