# UPI - Government Payments

Yes, many municipal corporations and state governments accept UPI payments for property tax. You can pay through the official portal or app by choosing UPI as the payment method. You may need to enter your property tax ID and then use UPI to complete the payment.

Visit the municipal corporation's portal, go to property tax payment, select UPI, and follow the instructions. You can also use your UPI app to scan the QR code on the portal.

The user learns that UPI can be used for property tax payments.
