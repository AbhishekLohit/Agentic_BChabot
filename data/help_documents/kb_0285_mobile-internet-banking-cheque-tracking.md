# Mobile & Internet Banking - Cheque Tracking

Yes, many banks allow you to track the status of a cheque issued by you through net banking. You can see if a cheque has been presented for clearing, the date of presentation, and if it has been paid or returned. Look for 'Cheque Status' or 'Cheque Inquiry' in your net banking portal. You may need to enter the cheque number and the amount.

Log in to your net banking portal. Navigate to the 'Services' or 'Transactions' section. Look for 'Cheque Status' or 'Cheque Inquiry'. Enter the cheque number, the account number, and the amount. Submit to get the current status of the cheque. If this feature is not available, you can call the bank's customer care for the status.

The user learns how to track cheque status online through net banking.
