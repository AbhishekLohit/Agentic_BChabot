# Money Muling & Legal Risks

Banks' fraud detection systems flag unusual credit patterns — regular large credits from corporate accounts to personal accounts, especially for amounts inconsistent with the account's historical profile, trigger automated risk flags. This is a systemic issue affecting gig workers in India whose payment pattern doesn't match traditional salary credit norms. The freeze is typically preventive and can be resolved by providing employment documentation. As per RBI's KYC and Risk Classification guidelines for individual accounts.

Visit your bank branch with: (1) your employment/gig platform contract or offer letter, (2) your most recent payment advice from the employer, (3) a letter from your employer confirming the payment arrangement on their letterhead. Request the branch to update your account risk classification to 'salaried/gig worker' and unfreeze. If refused, escalate to the Banking Ombudsman.

User learns that unusual credit patterns trigger bank AML risk flags and knows how to provide employment evidence to resolve the freeze and update account classification.
