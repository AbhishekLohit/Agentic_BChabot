# UPI - Security

UPI PIN is a 4-6 digit password used to authorize UPI transactions. Your ATM PIN is used for ATM transactions and point-of-sale (POS) purchases. They are separate security credentials. You should never share either of them with anyone. UPI PIN is set up during the UPI registration process and can be reset using your debit card details.

Keep your UPI PIN and ATM PIN confidential. Do not share them with anyone. If you forget your UPI PIN, reset it through your UPI app using your debit card.

The user learns the difference between UPI PIN and ATM PIN.
