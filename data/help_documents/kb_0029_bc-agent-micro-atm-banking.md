# BC Agent & Micro-ATM Banking

Yes, authorized BC agents can facilitate the opening of Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) accounts including Jan Dhan accounts at their kiosks. This is one of the primary purposes of the BC network under PMJDY. For KYC, the Aadhaar card and a self-attested photograph are the minimum requirements. If the customer has Aadhaar and their mobile number is linked to Aadhaar, e-KYC can be done on the spot through biometric authentication using your micro-ATM device. For customers without Aadhaar, a 'Small Account' can be opened with only a photograph and thumb impression before a bank official, with limited transaction capabilities.

1) Use your micro-ATM/BC device to initiate account opening through the bank's BC portal. 2) Collect Aadhaar number, live photograph, and mobile number. 3) Conduct Aadhaar-based eKYC biometric authentication. 4) Ensure the RuPay card request is included in the account opening form. 5) Guide the customer to link the account for DBT/PMJDY benefits by visiting the nearest CSC or doing it through your device if enabled. 6) Contact your bank's BC support desk if you face technical issues.

BC agents understand they can open BSBD/Jan Dhan accounts using Aadhaar eKYC at their kiosks and the documents required, enabling last-mile account opening.
