# Mobile & Internet Banking - Deposit Management

Yes, you can set up or disable automatic renewal of fixed deposits (FD) through net banking. Look for the 'Auto Renewal' or 'Renewal' option for your FD. You can choose to renew the FD for the same or different tenure. The bank will automatically renew the FD at maturity.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Deposits' section. Select the FD you want to manage. Look for 'Auto Renewal' or 'Renewal'. Enable or disable the option. If enabled, choose the tenure for renewal. Save your preference.

The user learns how to manage automatic FD renewal online.
