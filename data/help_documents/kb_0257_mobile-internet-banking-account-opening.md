# Mobile & Internet Banking - Account Opening

Yes, you can open a new account, including a salary account, through net banking or the mobile app. Most banks offer a 'Open Account' or 'New Account' option on their portal. The process is simple and requires basic KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar). If you are opening a salary account, you may also need to provide your company's name and employee ID. The account opening is often completed within minutes through Video KYC.

Log in to your net banking portal (if you already have a relationship with the bank). Look for the 'Open Account' or 'New Savings Account' option. Choose the 'Salary Account' type. Fill in your details and upload the KYC documents. Complete the Video KYC call. Your account will be opened instantly. You will receive the account details by SMS and email.

The user learns that new accounts can be opened online through net banking.
