# Merchant Payments - Educational Services

For music lessons, you can collect payments via UPI using a QR code or payment link. Display your UPI QR code in your studio. For remote students, send a payment link via WhatsApp or email. If you have regular students, set up UPI AutoPay for monthly fees. This makes collection easy and reduces follow-ups. Ensure you have a clear refund and cancellation policy.

Generate a merchant UPI QR code and display it in your studio. For remote students, use payment links. For regular students, set up UPI AutoPay. Send reminders before the due date. If a student cancels, process a refund via UPI. Maintain a record of all payments.
