# KYC and Identity Verification - Re-KYC for High-Value

For high-value customers, re-KYC is required every 2 years . You need to provide the same set of documents as at the time of opening the account: identity proof (Aadhaar, passport, DL, voter ID), address proof (current), and PAN. If there is no change in your information, you can provide a self-declaration. If there is a change, you need to submit updated documents. The process can be completed online via net banking or video KYC . High-value accounts are typically those with large transaction volumes or NRIs.

Log in to your net banking. Check if there's a KYC update option. If available, update your details and upload documents. If the bank requires a branch visit, schedule an appointment. Carry your Aadhaar, PAN, and current address proof. The update will be processed within a few days.
