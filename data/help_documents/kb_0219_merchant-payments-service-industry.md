# Merchant Payments - Service Industry

For home cleaning, you can accept UPI payments after the service is complete. Your staff can carry a mobile QR code or send a payment link to the customer. You can also set up a system where the customer pays online before the service. For regular customers, set up UPI AutoPay for weekly or monthly cleaning. Ensure your staff is trained to handle UPI payments and can assist customers.
