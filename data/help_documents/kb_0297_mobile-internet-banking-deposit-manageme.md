# Mobile & Internet Banking - Deposit Management

Yes, you can view all your Fixed Deposits (FDs) and their details in net banking. Under the 'Deposits' or 'My Accounts' section, you will see a list of your FDs. You can check the deposit amount, interest rate, start date, maturity date, and maturity amount. You can also generate an e-FD certificate online and renew the FD at maturity.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Deposits' or 'Accounts' section. Your FDs will be listed. Click on a specific FD to view the details. You will see the maturity date and other information. You can also use the 'Renew FD' option to renew it before or on maturity.

The user learns how to view fixed deposit details and maturity date through net banking.
