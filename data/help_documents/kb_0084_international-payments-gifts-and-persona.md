# International Payments - Gifts and Personal Transfers

Sending a gift via UPI directly to the USA is not possible as UPI is currently a domestic system. However, you can use UPI to transfer money from your bank account to an international money transfer service like Wise, PayPal, or Western Union. These services then convert the money and send it to your cousin's bank account or digital wallet in the USA. For a small gift, PayPal is convenient; for larger amounts, Wise often offers better exchange rates and lower fees . Keep in mind the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) limit of USD 250,000 per financial year, which covers all your international remittances .

Choose a transfer service like Wise or PayPal. Create an account and verify your identity. Initiate a transfer using your Indian bank account or UPI as the funding source. Enter your cousin's bank account details or their email/phone for PayPal. The service will show you the total cost, exchange rate, and estimated delivery time. Confirm and send. Your cousin will receive the money in USD, typically within minutes to a couple of days.

The user learns that UPI is not for international transfers but can be used to fund specialized services for this purpose, and is aware of the LRS limit.
