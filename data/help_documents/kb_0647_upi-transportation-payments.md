# UPI - Transportation Payments

Yes, you can use UPI to pay for cab rides on Ola and Uber. Both apps have UPI as a payment option. You can link your UPI ID or choose UPI during checkout. You will be redirected to your UPI app to confirm the payment. UPI is widely accepted for ride payments.

Open the Ola/Uber app, go to payment options, select UPI, and link your UPI ID. For each ride, choose UPI as the payment method and authorize the payment.

The user learns that UPI can be used for cab payments.
