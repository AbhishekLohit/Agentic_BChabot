# KYC and Identity Verification - Small Account

Yes, she can open a 'Small Account' as per RBI guidelines . This account is specifically designed for individuals without any Officially Valid Documents. The bank will take a self-attested photograph and the bank officer will certify her signature or thumb impression. The account has a 12-month validity, with a maximum balance of ₹50,000 and total annual credits of ₹1 lakh . She can use this account to receive wages via UPI or bank transfer. After 12 months, she can either submit documents or continue with a regular KYC.

Visit a bank branch with your domestic help. Inform the officer about opening a 'Small Account'. The bank will help with the form. The officer will certify the signature/thumb impression. The account will be opened. You can start crediting her wages to this account immediately.
