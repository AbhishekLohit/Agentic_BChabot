# Mobile & Internet Banking - Savings Management

The Auto Sweep feature allows you to automatically transfer excess money from your savings account to a Fixed Deposit (FD) once your account balance exceeds a certain limit. This helps you earn higher interest on the surplus. You can set up Auto Sweep through net banking by selecting the source account, the threshold limit, and the FD tenure.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Deposits' or 'Savings' section. Select 'Auto Sweep' or 'Sweep-in'. Choose the savings account. Set the threshold limit (e.g., ₹50,000). Set the FD tenure (e.g., 1 year). The bank will create a FD for the surplus amount above the threshold. You can also set up reverse sweep for automatic FD closure.
