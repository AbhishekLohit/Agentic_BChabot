# Merchant Payments - Gym Memberships

For gym memberships, you can use UPI AutoPay to set up recurring payments. The customer will authorize a mandate for a fixed amount each month. You can also use a payment gateway that supports recurring UPI payments. This automates the payment collection and reduces manual follow-ups. Ensure you have a clear cancellation policy and inform customers how to cancel the mandate.

Contact your payment aggregator to enable UPI AutoPay. For each customer, send them a mandate link. They will approve the mandate once. After that, payments will be automatically deducted. Send reminders to customers before the due date. If a customer cancels, process the cancellation through your gateway.
