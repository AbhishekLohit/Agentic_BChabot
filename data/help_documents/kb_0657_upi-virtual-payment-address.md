# UPI - Virtual Payment Address

Yes, UPI allows you to send money to a Virtual Payment Address (VPA), which is the UPI ID. The VPA is linked to a bank account, so when you send money to the VPA, it is credited to the associated bank account. You don't need to know the beneficiary's bank account number or IFSC for UPI transactions.

Enter the beneficiary's VPA (UPI ID) in your UPI app, enter the amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN. The money will be sent to their linked bank account.

The user learns that UPI uses VPA for transactions.
