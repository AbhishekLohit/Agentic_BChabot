# International Payments & FEMA

UPI is primarily a domestic India payment system. NPCI has expanded UPI internationally to select countries (UAE, Singapore, Bhutan, Nepal, France, UK, and others) but this is for Indian tourists — it allows UPI at specific merchant QR codes that accept international UPI. For day-to-day UK living (rent, groceries, transport), you will need a UK bank account. UK challenger banks (Monzo, Starling) can be opened digitally in a few days after arrival with just your passport. As per NPCI's UPI One World international expansion documentation.

Before departing: load a multi-currency travel card (Niyo Global, INR-to-GBP) for first-week expenses. Upon arrival in UK: open a Monzo or Starling account using your passport (no UK credit history needed). Check if your UK university has partnered with specific banks for student accounts. Keep your Indian account active for receiving remittances from family.

User learns UPI's limited international applicability and knows the fastest path to UK banking (Monzo/Starling digital account) to manage daily UK expenses as an international student.
