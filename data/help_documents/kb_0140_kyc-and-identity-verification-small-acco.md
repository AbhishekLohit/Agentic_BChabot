# KYC and Identity Verification - Small Account

Yes, if you do not possess any Officially Valid Document (OVD), you can still open a 'Small Account' with a bank. Under RBI guidelines, the bank will obtain a self-attested photograph from you, and a designated bank officer will certify your signature or thumb impression . This account remains operational for a period of 12 months. There are conditions: the total credits in a financial year cannot exceed ₹1 lakh, and the total balance at any time cannot exceed ₹50,000 . You can only make withdrawals from India. This account is designed for financial inclusion.

Visit a bank branch. Inform the bank officer that you want to open a 'Small Account'. Carry a passport-sized photograph. The bank will help you fill out the form and get the officer's certification. Your account will be opened. Remember, you have 12 months to submit a regular OVD to keep the account active beyond that period.
