# UPI - Subscription Payments

Yes, many professional bodies accept UPI for membership fees. You can pay through their portal by choosing UPI as the payment method. Some also provide a QR code that you can scan and pay.

Visit the professional body's portal, go to membership payment, select UPI, and follow the instructions. Alternatively, scan the QR code if provided.

The user learns that UPI can be used for professional membership fees.
