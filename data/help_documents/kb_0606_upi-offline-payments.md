# UPI - Offline Payments

Yes, UPI can be used for offline payments at physical stores. You can either scan the merchant's QR code using your UPI app or use the 'Pay via UPI' option. Merchants display a QR code that you can scan and enter the amount to pay. UPI is widely accepted at most retail stores, restaurants, and street vendors.

Open your UPI app, scan the merchant's QR code, enter the amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN. Make sure the merchant's QR code is UPI-enabled.

The user learns that UPI can be used for offline payments.
