# UPI - Education Payments

Yes, many schools have online portals where you can pay fees using UPI. You can choose UPI as the payment method, enter your UPI ID, and confirm the payment. Some schools also provide a QR code that you can scan and pay.

Visit the school's fee payment portal, choose UPI, enter your UPI ID or scan the QR code, and confirm the payment.

The user learns that school fees can be paid online via UPI.
