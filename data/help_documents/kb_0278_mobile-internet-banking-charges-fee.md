# Mobile & Internet Banking - Charges & Fee

Cheque bounce charges are penalties levied by banks when a cheque is returned unpaid due to insufficient funds or other reasons. The charges vary by bank, typically ranging from ₹250 to ₹500 per instance. Most banks publish their schedule of charges on their website and in the net banking portal. You can usually find this under 'Service Charges' or 'Schedule of Charges'.

Log in to your net banking portal. Look for 'Service Charges', 'Fees', or 'Schedule of Charges'. Download the PDF to see the cheque bounce charges. Always ensure you have sufficient funds before issuing a cheque to avoid these charges.
