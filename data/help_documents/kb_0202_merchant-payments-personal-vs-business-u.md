# Merchant Payments - Personal vs Business UPI

If a customer pays to your personal UPI ID, the money will be credited to your personal bank account. You need to manually track this payment and link it to the customer's order. To avoid this, always use your business UPI ID for business transactions. You can also display both UPI IDs, but clearly mark which one is for business payments. For existing payments, check your personal account statement and match it with the customer's order.

Check your personal bank account statement to confirm the payment. Link it to the customer's order manually. To prevent this in the future, display your business UPI ID prominently. You can also disable your personal UPI ID for payments or set a note that says 'For personal use only'.

The merchant learns how to handle payments made to personal UPI IDs.
