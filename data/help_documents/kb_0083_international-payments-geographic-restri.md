# International Payments - Geographic Restrictions

Yes, sending a monetary gift to Saudi Arabia is generally allowed under the LRS . Saudi Arabia is not a prohibited country under the LRS regulations. The remittance must be for a permissible purpose (gift/donation) and will be subject to the overall LRS limit of USD 250,000 per financial year. You will need a PAN card and a valid purpose code (S1300 for gifts). However, you should check with your bank or the remittance service provider, as some banks may have their own internal compliance policies for remittances to certain middle-eastern countries, which might require additional scrutiny .

Choose a remittance service like Western Union or Wise, or use your bank's outward remittance service. Provide the recipient's full name, bank account number, and the bank's SWIFT/BIC code. Ensure you select the 'Gift' or 'Donation' purpose category. The transfer will be processed subject to the LRS limits and TCS rules .

The user learns that sending gifts to Saudi Arabia is permitted under LRS, though they must be aware of the limits and potential bank-specific compliance checks.
