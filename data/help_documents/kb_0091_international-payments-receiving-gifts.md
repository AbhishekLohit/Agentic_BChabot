# International Payments - Receiving Gifts

Receiving a gift from abroad into your Indian bank account is a straightforward process. Your parents in the UK can send the money via SWIFT transfer, Wise, Western Union, or PayPal . For receiving, a SWIFT transfer is the standard method. You need to provide your parents with your bank account number, IFSC code, and the bank's SWIFT/BIC code . There are no limits on how much you can receive in India as a gift, but large sums might attract the bank's attention and could have income tax implications for you as per Indian tax laws if considered income. You will receive a credit in your account, and the bank will generate a Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) as proof of receipt, which is useful for tax purposes .

Provide your parents with the following: Account holder name (as per bank records), Account number, Bank name and branch, IFSC Code, SWIFT/BIC Code. They can initiate the transfer from their bank or via Wise. Once the money is sent, it will typically take 1-5 working days to arrive via SWIFT. You can track the status with your bank using the UTR .
