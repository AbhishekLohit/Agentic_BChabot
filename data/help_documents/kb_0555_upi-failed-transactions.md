# UPI - Failed Transactions

If you received money via UPI but it's not reflecting in your bank balance, it could be due to a technical glitch or a failed transaction. Check your UPI app for the transaction status. If it shows 'Success' but your balance hasn't updated, wait for a few hours. If the issue persists, contact your bank with the transaction reference number.

Check the transaction status in your UPI app. Note the transaction ID. If the issue persists, contact your bank's customer support and provide the transaction details.

The user learns how to handle UPI credit issues.
