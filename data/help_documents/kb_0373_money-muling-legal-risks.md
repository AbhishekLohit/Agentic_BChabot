# Money Muling & Legal Risks

P2P cryptocurrency trading via UPI in India operates in a legally grey zone. While owning crypto is not illegal per se, using UPI for P2P crypto trades has led to multiple bank account freezes because: (1) NPCI issued advisories to banks to monitor accounts used for crypto transactions, (2) many buyers on P2P platforms are scam victims transferring their last savings, which links your account to fraud investigations, (3) the large volume of strangers' payments triggers AML flags. There is significant regulatory uncertainty. As per RBI's 2022 directive to banks on crypto-related transaction monitoring.

Stop P2P crypto trading on UPI immediately pending legal clarity. For your frozen account, engage a banking lawyer and file a formal grievance with the bank citing that you are not involved in fraud. Provide complete crypto trade records. If the bank does not respond within 30 days, file with the Banking Ombudsman. Consider using regulated crypto exchanges (CoinDCX, WazirX) instead of P2P.
