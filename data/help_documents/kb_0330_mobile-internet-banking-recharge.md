# Mobile & Internet Banking - Recharge

Yes, many mobile banking apps allow you to recharge your transit card (like Metro or Bus card). You can add the card details and recharge it from your bank account. This is a convenient feature for daily commuters.

Open your mobile banking app. Go to 'Payments' or 'Recharges'. Select 'Transit Card' or 'Metro Card'. Enter your card details (e.g., card number, UPI ID). Enter the recharge amount. Confirm and pay. The recharge will be processed and reflected on your card.

The user learns about transit card recharge through mobile banking.
