# Merchant Payments - Catering Services

For catering, you can collect advance payments via UPI using a payment link or QR code. Send a payment link for the advance amount. After the event, you can collect the final balance via another payment link or QR code. Ensure your pricing is clear. For large events, consider using a payment gateway with invoice generation. This makes it easy to track payments.

Generate a payment link for the advance amount and send it to the client. After the event, generate a new link for the final balance. Keep a record of all payments. If you have regular clients, consider setting up UPI AutoPay for recurring services. Maintain clear communication about payment schedules.

The merchant learns how to handle advance and final payments via UPI.
