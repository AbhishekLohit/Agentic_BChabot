# Merchant Payments - Technical Reversals

If a UPI payment is reversed due to a 'technical error', the funds will be credited back to the customer's account. This is an auto-reversal initiated by the system. You need to inform the customer and ask them to make the payment again. If you have already provided the goods or services, you may need to follow up with the customer for the payment. To prevent this, ensure your payment gateway is stable and your account is in good standing.

Check your settlement report to confirm the reversal. Inform the customer that the payment was reversed due to a technical error and ask them to pay again. Send them a new payment link or QR code. If the customer is not responsive, you may need to escalate the issue. Keep a record of the reversal for your accounting.

The merchant learns how to handle technical reversals.
