# UPI - Recurring Payments

Yes, NPCI has introduced UPI AutoPay for recurring payments. You can set up an e-mandate for recurring payments like Netflix, insurance premiums, or loan EMIs. The maximum amount per transaction is ₹5,000 for most banks, but some banks allow up to ₹1 lakh. You need to authorize the mandate once, and subsequent payments are automatically debited.

Look for the 'UPI AutoPay' option in your UPI app. Set up the mandate with the merchant, choose the frequency and amount, and authenticate with your UPI PIN.
