# KYC & Compliance

Yes, a proprietary concern can open a current account in the business name with UPI functionality. Banks require additional business KYC for a proprietorship account including: sole proprietor identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN), business existence proof (GST certificate, shop and establishment registration, trade license, or utility bill in business name), business address proof, and a declaration of sole proprietorship. The UPI VPA can be registered in the business name (e.g., businessname@hdfc). As per RBI's KYC Master Directions for Proprietary Concern accounts.

Visit a bank branch with: Aadhaar and PAN of the proprietor, one business address proof (GST certificate or trade license), a cancelled cheque, and a filled account opening form. For UPI, the mobile number used must be the proprietor's registered number. After account opening, register UPI normally using the business's account IFSC.

User learns the specific KYC requirements for proprietorship current accounts and knows they can have a business-named UPI handle for professional transactions.
