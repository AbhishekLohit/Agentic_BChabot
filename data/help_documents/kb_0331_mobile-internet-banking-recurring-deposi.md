# Mobile & Internet Banking - Recurring Deposit

Yes, you can open a Recurring Deposit (RD) account through net banking. Similar to Fixed Deposits, you can set up an RD online. You need to provide the amount to be deposited each month, the tenure, and the account from which the amount will be debited. The RD will be created instantly. You can also set up a standing instruction for automatic monthly debits.

Log in to your net banking portal. Look for the 'Deposits' or 'Recurring Deposit' section. Select 'Open RD' or 'Create RD'. Enter the monthly deposit amount, the tenure, and select the debit account. Choose the interest payout option. Confirm the details and submit. The RD will be created instantly. You can also set up a standing instruction for auto-debit.
