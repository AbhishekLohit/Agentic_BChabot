# UPI - Entertainment Payments

Yes, BookMyShow and other ticketing platforms accept UPI payments. You can choose UPI at checkout, enter your UPI ID, and confirm the payment. UPI is widely accepted for movie, event, and concert tickets.

On BookMyShow, select UPI as the payment option. Enter your UPI ID or scan the QR code, and confirm the payment.

The user learns that UPI is accepted for movie ticket bookings.
