# Money Muling & Legal Risks

Foreign inward remittances for freelance/export services are legitimate and regulated under FEMA. Your bank is required to verify the source per FEMA regulations and RBI's KYC guidelines for foreign currency receipts. This is standard Enhanced Due Diligence for FOREX credits, not harassment. You are entitled to receive freelance foreign income in your resident savings account if it qualifies as export of services under FEMA. As per FEMA Section 2(e) on current account transactions and RBI's Master Direction on Foreign Currency Accounts.

Provide your bank with: (1) freelance service agreement or email confirmation from the foreign client, (2) invoice raised to the foreign client, (3) FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) — request this from your bank once they process the funds. This documentation completes FEMA compliance. Consider opening an EEFC account if you regularly receive large foreign income.
