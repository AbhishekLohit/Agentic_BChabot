# Mobile & Internet Banking - Regional Language Support

Yes, many banks offer regional language support in their mobile banking apps to enhance accessibility. For example, some banks provide the app interface in Tamil, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, and other languages. You can change the language in the app settings. This feature is part of the RBI's financial inclusion initiative to make banking accessible to non-English speakers.

Open your bank's mobile app. Go to 'Settings' or 'Profile'. Look for 'Language' or 'App Language'. Select your preferred language (e.g., Tamil). The app interface will change to that language. All menus, options, and messages will be displayed in the selected language.

The user learns about the regional language support in mobile banking apps and how to change the language.
