# Mobile & Internet Banking - Deposit Management

Yes, you can close a fixed deposit prematurely through net banking. Look for the 'Close FD' or 'Premature Withdrawal' option in the deposits section. You need to select the FD you want to close. The bank will calculate the premature withdrawal penalty (if any) and credit the amount to your savings account instantly. The process is simple but ensure you are aware of the penalty.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Deposits' section. Select the FD you want to close. Click on 'Premature Withdrawal' or 'Close FD'. The system will show you the amount you will receive after applying penalties. Confirm the closure. The amount will be credited to your savings account immediately.
