# Mobile & Internet Banking - Payments

Yes, you can make QR code payments through net banking. You can scan a merchant's QR code using your mobile banking app and make a payment. Alternatively, you can use the 'Pay by QR' feature in some net banking portals that allow you to upload a QR code image.

Open your mobile banking app. Scan the merchant's QR code. Enter the amount and your MPIN. The payment will be made. In net banking, look for the 'QR Payment' option and upload the QR code image.

The user learns about QR code payment features through digital banking.
