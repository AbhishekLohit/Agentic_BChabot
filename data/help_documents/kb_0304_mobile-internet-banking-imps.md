# Mobile & Internet Banking - IMPS

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) is available 24x7 and is typically enabled by default for net banking users. If you can send money through NEFT or RTGS, you can usually send through IMPS as well. To check if IMPS is enabled, log in to net banking and try to add a beneficiary with 'IMPS' as the transfer type. If the option is available, it is enabled. Most banks automatically enable IMPS. You can also check the 'Fund Transfer' section to see if IMPS is listed as an option.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Fund Transfer' section. Look for an option that says 'IMPS' or 'Immediate Payment Service'. If you see it, your account is registered for IMPS. To send money, select the beneficiary, enter the amount, and choose 'IMPS' as the transfer mode. The transfer will be completed instantly. If IMPS is not listed, call your bank's customer care to activate it.
