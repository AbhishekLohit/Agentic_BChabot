# Merchant Payments - International Tourists

International tourists can pay you via UPI if they have a UPI One World wallet or a linked international card. NPCI has introduced UPI One World for foreign nationals, which allows them to use UPI by linking their international cards. As a merchant, you don't need to do anything different — your existing UPI QR code will work. The payment will be processed in INR, and you will receive the settlement in INR. If the tourist does not have UPI One World, they can use their international card via a payment gateway.

Display your UPI QR code prominently. International tourists with UPI One World can scan and pay. For those without it, you can also accept international card payments using a POS machine or a payment gateway like Razorpay. Inform your staff to guide tourists on using UPI if they are interested.
