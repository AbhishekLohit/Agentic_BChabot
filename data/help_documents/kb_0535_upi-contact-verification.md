# UPI - Contact Verification

When you enter a UPI ID or phone number, the UPI app fetches the name registered with that UPI ID from the bank. If the name shown is different from the name you have saved in your contacts, it could indicate that you have entered the wrong UPI ID, or the recipient has not updated their name with their bank. It could also be a sign of a potential fraud if someone is using a fake name. Always verify the name shown on the app before confirming the payment.

Before entering your UPI PIN, double-check the name that appears on the screen. If it does not match the person you intended to pay, cancel the transaction. Contact the recipient to verify their UPI ID. If the name is completely different, do not proceed with the payment. If the name is a minor variation (e.g., initials instead of full name), it might be fine, but always confirm with the recipient.

The user learns the importance of verifying the displayed name before making a UPI payment.
