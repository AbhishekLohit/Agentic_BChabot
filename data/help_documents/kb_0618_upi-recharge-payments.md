# UPI - Recharge Payments

Yes, you can recharge your DTH service using UPI. Most UPI apps have a 'Recharge' section where you can select 'DTH', enter your subscriber ID, choose the plan, and pay using your UPI PIN. UPI is widely used for DTH recharges.

Open your UPI app, go to 'Recharge', select 'DTH', enter your subscriber ID, choose a plan, and pay.

The user learns that UPI can be used for DTH recharges.
