# Mobile & Internet Banking - Account Statements

Yes, you can export your transaction history to your email directly from net banking. Select the date range, choose the format (PDF/Excel), and enter your email ID. The bank will send the statement to your registered email. This is useful for record-keeping.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Accounts' section. Select 'Download Account Statement'. Choose the date range. Select the format. Enter your email ID (if not the default). Click on 'Send to Email'. The statement will be delivered to your email inbox.

The user learns how to export transaction history to email through net banking.
