# Mobile & Internet Banking - Statement Generation

You can get a mini statement (last 5-10 transactions) through SMS or USSD (*99#). However, a full detailed account statement for a specific period is usually not available via SMS or USSD. For a detailed statement, you need to use net banking or mobile banking. The mini statement via SMS/USSD is useful for a quick transaction update.

For a mini statement, send an SMS from your registered mobile number or dial *99# and follow the prompts. The mini statement will be sent to your phone. For a detailed statement, log in to your net banking or mobile banking app and download the statement.

The user learns the difference between SMS/USSD mini statements and detailed statements available in apps.
