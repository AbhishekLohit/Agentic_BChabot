# Mobile & Internet Banking - Profile Management

Yes, you can update your nominee details online through net banking. The process is similar to adding a nominee. You need to enter the nominee's details. Some banks may require you to confirm via OTP. The nomination is updated instantly in most cases.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to 'Profile' or 'Services'. Select 'Nomination' or 'Update Nominee'. Choose the account. Enter the nominee's details. Submit and confirm. The nomination will be updated in the system.
