# UPI - Subscription Payments

Yes, many online learning platforms like Coursera, Udemy, and Byju's accept UPI payments. You can choose UPI at checkout, enter your UPI ID, and confirm the payment. For recurring subscriptions, you can set up UPI AutoPay.

During checkout on the learning platform, select UPI as the payment method. Enter your UPI ID and confirm the payment. For recurring subscriptions, set up UPI AutoPay.

The user learns that UPI can be used for online learning subscriptions.
