# Merchant Payments - E-commerce Integration

For Shopify stores, the best UPI payment apps are: 1) Razorpay - offers seamless UPI integration with 'intent' flow and high success rates. 2) PayU - another reliable gateway with UPI support. 3) Instamojo - good for small businesses. 4) Cashfree - also popular for UPI. All these gateways offer Shopify apps that are easy to install. They support all major UPI apps and provide features like payment links, refunds, and settlement reports. Choose one based on your transaction volume and fees.

Go to the Shopify App Store and search for 'UPI payment gateway'. Compare the features and fees of Razorpay, PayU, Instamojo, and Cashfree. Install the app you choose. Follow the setup instructions to integrate UPI on your checkout page. Test the flow thoroughly before going live. Also, ensure the gateway supports both 'intent' and 'collect' flows for maximum customer convenience.
