# KYC & Compliance

RBI's KYC Master Directions include a comprehensive list of acceptable address proofs, which specifically includes 'Letter from village panchayat/gram panchayat' for rural customers where standard utility bills may not be available. Banks are legally obligated to accept gram panchayat certificates as address proof for KYC purposes. Branch staff may be unaware of this provision. As per RBI's KYC Master Directions (updated 2023) — Simplified Measures for Low-Risk Customers in Rural Areas.

Carry a printed copy of RBI's KYC Master Directions or the relevant paragraph on simplified KYC documents for rural areas to the bank branch. Specifically cite RBI Master Direction RBI/2015-16/70 (updated 2023), which lists Gram Panchayat letter as a valid address proof. If the branch refuses, escalate to the bank's nodal officer.

User learns that gram panchayat certificates are RBI-approved address proof documents and knows how to cite the regulation if a bank branch refuses to accept them.
