# Mobile & Internet Banking - Service Requests

Yes, most net banking portals have a 'Service Request' or 'My Requests' section where you can track the status of your service requests (e.g., cheque book, debit card, address change). You can see if the request is pending, processed, or closed.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Services' or 'Requests' section. Look for 'My Requests' or 'Request Status'. You will see a list of all your requests with their status. You can track the progress of each request.

The user learns how to track service requests online through net banking.
