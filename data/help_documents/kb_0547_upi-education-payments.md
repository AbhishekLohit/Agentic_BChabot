# UPI - Education Payments

Yes, many daycare centers accept UPI payments. You can pay by scanning the QR code provided by the daycare or by entering their UPI ID. Some daycares also have a payment portal where UPI is an option.

Check if your daycare has a UPI QR code or UPI ID. If they have a portal, choose UPI and follow the instructions. If not, ask the daycare for their UPI details.

The user learns that daycare fees can be paid via UPI.
