# Mobile & Internet Banking - Loan Management

Yes, most net banking portals have a 'Loan EMI Calculator' feature. You can enter the loan amount, interest rate, and tenure to calculate your EMI. This helps in planning your monthly budget and understanding the total interest payable.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Loans' section. Look for 'EMI Calculator' or 'Loan Calculator'. Enter your loan amount, tenure, and interest rate. The calculator will show your monthly EMI and the total interest. Use this to plan your repayment.
