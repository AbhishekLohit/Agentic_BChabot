# UPI - Travel Payments

UPI can only be used for domestic transactions in Indian rupees. You cannot directly use UPI to load or pay for a foreign currency travel card. However, you can use UPI to transfer money to your bank account and then use net banking or a forex service to load the travel card.

Load your foreign currency travel card through your bank's net banking or mobile app. You can first transfer money to your bank account using UPI, then use the bank's forex services to load the card.

The user learns that UPI is for domestic INR transactions only.
