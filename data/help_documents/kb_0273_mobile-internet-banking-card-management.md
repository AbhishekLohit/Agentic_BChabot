# Mobile & Internet Banking - Card Management

Yes, you can block your debit card through net banking or mobile banking. Most banks offer a 'Block Card' or 'Card Control' feature. You can temporarily or permanently block your debit card. The process is quick and secure. If you have lost your card, blocking it immediately prevents unauthorized use. You can also unblock it later if needed. Some banks offer a temporary block option.

Log in to your net banking portal. Navigate to the 'Cards' or 'Debit Card' section. Select the card you want to block. Click on 'Block Card' or 'Temporary Block'. You will be asked to confirm the action. Enter your transaction password or OTP to authorize the block. The card will be blocked instantly. You can also request a replacement card through the same portal.

The user learns the process to block a debit card through net banking for security reasons.
