# Transaction Failures & Refunds

Merchant-initiated UPI refunds can be delayed due to: (1) the merchant initiating the refund via their payment gateway which takes 5–7 business days, (2) NPCI processing delay, or (3) a refund that was initiated but failed and was not retried. RBI mandates that online refunds for failed or cancelled digital transactions must be credited within 5 business days. A 10-day wait for a refund of a confirmed cancellation warrants formal escalation to both the merchant and the bank. As per RBI's Payment Aggregator and Payment Gateway Circular (2021).

Request a 'Refund Reference Number' (RRN) from the merchant — if they initiated a refund, this number exists. Provide this RRN to your bank, who can trace the refund through the payment aggregator. If neither can trace it, file a complaint with the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000) and the RBI Ombudsman.

User learns to request the Refund Reference Number as evidence that a refund was actually initiated, and uses it to trace between merchant, payment gateway, and bank.
