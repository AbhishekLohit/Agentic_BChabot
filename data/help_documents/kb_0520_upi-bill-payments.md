# UPI - Bill Payments

UPI for credit card payments uses the BBPS (Bharat Bill Payment System) platform. The failure could be due to the credit card issuer not being onboarded on BBPS, or your UPI app not supporting the specific credit card issuer. Not all credit cards are on BBPS yet. Alternatively, the payment could exceed your daily UPI limit (usually ₹1 lakh for normal transactions). The issue could also be a temporary NPCI network error. You can try using the credit card issuer's own app or website for the payment, or use net banking.

Check if your credit card issuer is on the BBPS platform. Use the credit card issuer's mobile app to make the payment. Alternatively, use net banking or debit card to pay the bill. If the issue is the UPI limit, make multiple smaller payments. If it persists, contact your bank's customer care and report the issue.

The user learns about BBPS integration with UPI, why certain credit card payments fail, and alternatives to pay credit card bills.
