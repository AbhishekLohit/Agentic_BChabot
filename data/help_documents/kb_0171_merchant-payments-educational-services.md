# Merchant Payments - Educational Services

For tutoring services, UPI is an excellent option. You can generate a UPI QR code and display it on your website, social media, or in your classroom. For remote students, you can send a UPI payment link via WhatsApp or email. If you have recurring monthly fees, you can set up UPI AutoPay for automatic payments. This simplifies collection and reduces follow-ups. You can also use a payment gateway to send invoices with a UPI payment button.

Generate a merchant UPI QR code. Display it in your classroom, on your website, and in WhatsApp groups. For remote students, use payment links. For regular students, set up UPI AutoPay. Send a reminder message a few days before the due date with a payment link. This will streamline your fee collection.
