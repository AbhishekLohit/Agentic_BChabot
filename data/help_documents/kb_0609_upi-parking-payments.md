# UPI - Parking Payments

Yes, many parking lots and malls accept UPI for parking fees. You can scan the QR code displayed at the parking exit or entrance, enter the parking fee, and pay using your UPI PIN. UPI is widely used for parking payments.

Scan the parking QR code using your UPI app, enter the fee, and confirm with your UPI PIN.

The user learns that UPI can be used for parking payments.
