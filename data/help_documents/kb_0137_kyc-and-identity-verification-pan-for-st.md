# KYC and Identity Verification - PAN for Students

For opening a basic savings account (BSBDA) or a student account, PAN may not be mandatory if the account is for receiving scholarships or pocket money. However, if the total credits in a financial year exceed ₹50,000, or if you want to invest in mutual funds or other securities, PAN is mandatory . You can submit Form 60 as a substitute, but some banks may still prefer PAN for a student account. It's advisable to apply for a PAN card even as a student, as it will be useful for future financial transactions.

If you don't have PAN, submit Form 60 to the bank. If the bank insists on PAN, apply for a PAN card online through the NSDL or UTIITSL portal. It costs around ₹100 and takes 7-10 days. Once you have PAN, submit it to the bank for KYC.
