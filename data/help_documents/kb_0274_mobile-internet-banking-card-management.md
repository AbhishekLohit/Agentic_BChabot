# Mobile & Internet Banking - Card Management

Yes, you can check your debit/credit card loyalty or reward points in net banking. Look for 'Reward Points', 'Loyalty Points', or 'Card Rewards' in the cards section. You can view your points balance, redemption options, and transaction history. You can also redeem your points for vouchers or cashback through the portal.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Cards' section. Select your debit or credit card. Look for 'Reward Points' or 'Loyalty Points'. You will see your points balance. You can also view the redemption options and redeem your points online.
