# Merchant Payments - Taxi Services

For taxi services, you can display a UPI QR code inside the taxi. The passenger can scan the QR code at the end of the ride and pay the exact fare. You can also generate a dynamic QR code that shows the fare amount. For advance bookings, you can send a payment link. Ensure your driver has a mobile device with the UPI app to receive confirmation of payment.

Place a UPI QR code sticker inside the taxi. For each ride, calculate the fare and inform the passenger. The passenger scans the QR code and enters the amount. Ensure the driver receives a notification of successful payment. For online bookings, use payment links. Train your drivers to handle UPI payments and provide change if needed.

The merchant learns how to use UPI for taxi service payments.
