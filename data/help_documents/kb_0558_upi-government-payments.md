# UPI - Government Payments

Yes, many government exam portals like UPSC, SSC, and banking exams accept UPI for application fees. You can choose UPI as the payment method during the fee payment step and complete the transaction using your UPI PIN.

On the exam application portal, select UPI as the payment method. Enter your UPI ID and confirm the payment.

The user learns that UPI can be used for government exam fees.
