# Mobile & Internet Banking - Notifications

Yes, mobile banking apps provide push notifications for every transaction. You can enable this in the app settings. You can also choose to receive notifications for both debit and credit transactions, and you can set a minimum amount for which you want to receive alerts. This helps in real-time monitoring of your account.

Open your mobile banking app. Go to 'Settings' or 'Notifications'. Enable 'Transaction Alerts' or 'Push Notifications'. You can also set a threshold (e.g., get alerts for transactions above ₹500). Save your preferences. You will now receive notifications for all transactions.

The user learns how to enable transaction notifications in the mobile banking app.
