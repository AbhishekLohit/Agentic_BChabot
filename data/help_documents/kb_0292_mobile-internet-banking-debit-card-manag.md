# Mobile & Internet Banking - Debit Card Management

Yes, if your debit card supports contactless payments (NFC), you can manage its settings through net banking. You can enable or disable the contactless feature, set a transaction limit for contactless payments (usually up to ₹5,000), and view your contactless transaction history. This is available in the 'Cards' management section.

Log in to your net banking portal. Go to the 'Cards' or 'Debit Card' section. Select your debit card. Look for 'Contactless Payments', 'NFC', or 'Tap to Pay'. You can enable/disable the feature and set a transaction limit. The changes will be applied to your card.
