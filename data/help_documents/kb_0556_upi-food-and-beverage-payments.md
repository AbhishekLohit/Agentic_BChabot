# UPI - Food and Beverage Payments

Yes, most restaurants in India accept UPI payments. You can scan the restaurant's QR code at the table or at the billing counter, enter the bill amount, and pay using your UPI PIN. Many restaurants also have UPI IDs displayed.

Scan the restaurant's QR code using your UPI app, enter the bill amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN. Alternatively, ask the restaurant for their UPI ID.

The user learns that UPI is widely accepted at restaurants.
