# Merchant Payments - Advance Payments

For photography services, you can collect advance payments via UPI using a payment link or QR code. Send a payment link to the client with the exact advance amount. You can also generate a QR code and send it via WhatsApp or email. For repeat clients, you can set up a standard UPI ID. Ensure you have a clear cancellation policy and process refunds through UPI if needed.

Generate a payment link or QR code for the advance amount. Send it to the client via WhatsApp or email. Once the payment is received, confirm the booking. If the client cancels, process a refund via UPI. Keep a record of all payments for accounting and tax purposes.

The merchant learns how to collect advance payments via UPI.
