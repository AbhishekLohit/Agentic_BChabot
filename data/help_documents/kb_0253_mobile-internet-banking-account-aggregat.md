# Mobile & Internet Banking - Account Aggregator

The Account Aggregator feature allows you to view all your accounts (across different banks and financial institutions) in a single mobile app. You can consent to share your financial data securely. This is a new RBI-backed initiative to help you manage your finances better.

If your bank supports the Account Aggregator framework, you can download a third-party app (like Perfios, Finvu). Log in and provide your consent. Your accounts will be linked, and you will get a consolidated view.

The user learns about the Account Aggregator framework and its benefits.
