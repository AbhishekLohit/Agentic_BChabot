# UPI - Transaction History

You can check your UPI transaction history directly in your UPI app. Most apps have a 'Transactions' or 'History' section where you can see all past transactions with details like amount, date, and UPI ID. You can also download a statement or export the history for your records.

Open your UPI app, go to the 'Transactions' tab, and view your history. If you need a detailed statement, check if your app allows downloading a PDF or CSV.

The user learns how to view UPI transaction history.
