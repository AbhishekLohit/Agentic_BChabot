# UPI - Bill Payments

Yes, you can pay your water bill using UPI. Most UPI apps have a 'Bill Payments' section where you can select 'Water' and enter your consumer number or account number. The bill details are fetched, and you can pay using your UPI PIN.

Open your UPI app, go to 'Bill Payments', select 'Water', enter your consumer number, fetch the bill, and pay.

The user learns that UPI can be used for water bill payments.
