# Money Transfers - NEFT

She should use NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer). NEFT is available 24/7 through internet banking and mobile banking. The RBI has directed banks not to charge savings account holders for online NEFT transfers, making it free. Transactions are processed in hourly batches, so it may take up to 2 hours for you to receive the money. She will need your bank account number and IFSC code.

Your mother should log in to her bank's internet banking. Go to 'Fund Transfer' and select 'NEFT'. Enter your account number and IFSC code. Enter the amount and confirm. The funds will be credited to your account in the next NEFT batch.

The user learns that NEFT is a free and reliable method for online transfers from savings accounts.
