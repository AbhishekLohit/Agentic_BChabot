# Merchant Payments - Remote Payments

For food delivery, you can use UPI payment links or 'collect request' features. When a customer places an order, you can generate a payment link with the exact amount and send it via SMS or WhatsApp. The customer can click the link and pay using their UPI app. You can also use a dynamic QR code that shows the exact amount, which the customer can scan from their phone even if they are not physically present.

Integrate a payment gateway that generates UPI payment links. Use a system that automatically sends the payment link after the order is confirmed. Alternatively, use the UPI 'collect' feature manually. Ensure your delivery staff can handle customers who prefer to pay on delivery — offer UPI QR code scanning at the time of delivery.

The merchant learns how to accept UPI payments for remote orders.
