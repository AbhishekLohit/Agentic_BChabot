# UPI - Travel Payments

Yes, most airlines and travel portals like MakeMyTrip, Cleartrip, and Yatra accept UPI payments. You can choose UPI at checkout, enter your UPI ID, and confirm the payment. UPI is widely accepted for flight bookings.

During flight booking, select UPI as the payment option. Enter your UPI ID or scan the QR code, and confirm the payment.

The user learns that UPI is accepted for flight bookings.
