# UPI - Recharge Payments

Yes, you can recharge your mobile prepaid number using UPI. Most UPI apps have a 'Recharge' section where you can select 'Mobile', enter your phone number, choose the plan, and pay using your UPI PIN. UPI is widely used for mobile recharges.

Open your UPI app, go to 'Recharge', select 'Mobile', enter your phone number, choose a plan, and pay.

The user learns that UPI can be used for mobile recharges.
