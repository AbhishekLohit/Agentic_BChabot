# UPI - Education Payments

Yes, many schools and transport operators accept UPI for school bus fees. You can pay by scanning the QR code provided by the school or transport company, or by entering their UPI ID. The daily UPI limit of ₹1 lakh applies.

Ask the school or transport operator for their UPI ID or QR code. Open your UPI app, scan the QR code or enter the UPI ID, enter the amount, and confirm the payment.

The user learns that UPI can be used for school bus fee payments.
