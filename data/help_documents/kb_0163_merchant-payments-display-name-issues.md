# Merchant Payments - Display Name Issues

The name displayed on the customer's screen is the name linked to your bank account. If it is incorrect, you need to update your bank account name or open a business account. Some payment aggregators allow you to set a display name for QR codes. Contact your payment aggregator to see if this is possible. If not, you may need to open a current account in your business name.

Check the name linked to your bank account. If it is incorrect, update it with your bank. If you want to display a business name, open a business current account. Contact your payment aggregator to see if they allow a display name. If all else fails, display a small sign with your business name next to the QR code.

The merchant learns how to fix the display name issue on UPI QR codes.
